Generated by `automation/review/power_layout.py` from `hardware/compact-22mm/nubm44_driver.kicad_pcb` (SHA-256 `597e9a4dc4e7`). Numbers come from the routed copper; the screening criteria below select items for review and never open or close a gate. DC copper analysis does not model inductance, switching waveforms or temperature. Physical behaviour remains a Gate G (bench) item.
