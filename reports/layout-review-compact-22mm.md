## Screening results

- **REVIEW NECK-BAT_SPRING**: BAT_SPRING: 3.83 A/mm over the hottest 0.25 mm2 at 7.393 A (equivalent width 1.932 mm) exceeds the IPC-2221 20 K long-trace screen of 2.36 A/mm (3.133 mm) by x1.62 at [-1.9, -4.1] on B.Cu.
- **REVIEW VIA-VBAT**: VBAT: 2 via barrel segment(s) carry more than 1.0 A (max 1.412 A).
- **REVIEW NECK-SW1**: SW1: 3.58 A/mm over the hottest 0.25 mm2 at 7.418 A (equivalent width 2.071 mm) exceeds the IPC-2221 20 K long-trace screen of 2.36 A/mm (3.148 mm) by x1.52 at [-1.15, -4.9] on F.Cu.
- **REVIEW NECK-SW2**: SW2: 11.43 A/mm over the hottest 0.25 mm2 at 7.418 A (equivalent width 0.649 mm) exceeds the IPC-2221 20 K long-trace screen of 2.36 A/mm (3.148 mm) by x4.85 at [0.7, 0.7] on F.Cu.
- **REVIEW NECK-VOUT**: VOUT: 4.94 A/mm over the hottest 0.25 mm2 at 4.0 A (equivalent width 0.81 mm) exceeds the IPC-2221 20 K long-trace screen of 2.98 A/mm (1.343 mm) by x1.66 at [1.45, 5.35] on F.Cu.
- **REVIEW COPPER-LOSS**: Routed copper at 75 C dissipates 44 mW more than the loss model allows at the 4.0 V nominal point (switch-node copper is not in the model); estimated efficiency 94.588 % -> 94.365 %.
- **REVIEW GND-OFFSET**: DC ground offsets shift the regulated current by 30.022 mA (0.7506 %).
- **REVIEW DRIVE-DR1H**: DR1H (buck high-side gate) changes layer through 2 via(s); route 15.5 mm for a 8.39 mm pad spacing, loop-area estimate 15.0 mm2 against SW1.
- **REVIEW DRIVE-DR1L**: DR1L (buck low-side gate) changes layer through 2 via(s); route 11.88 mm for a 7.91 mm pad spacing, loop-area estimate 4.09 mm2 against GND.
