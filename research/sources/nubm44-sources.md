  - BeamQ and Odicforce NUBM44 listings: not fetched before the usage limit, so their power points (search snippet: 5.2 W @ 3 A, 6 W @ 3.5 A, 6.7 W @ 4 A, 7.3 W @ 4.5 A) are **unverified and not used**.
